### Product and business model
A personal AI for everyday conversation, documents, planning, records and authorized tasks. Core access costs CNY 1/month: CNY 12 over twelve monthly payments, or CNY 9.90 paid annually. Core functionality is available to every subscriber. Optional identity and visual personalization does not gate core use.

The first 90-day operating target is 100,000 new payers, with a 200,000 growth case and later million, ten-million and 100-million stages. The base monthly-payment model generates CNY 144,800 subscriptions and 99,500 personalization revenue, against 175,252 economic costs, leaving 69,048. Pricing, retention, attachment and costs are forward-looking assumptions.

## 03 Product & user journey

Connect a request to a usable output and follow-through.

| Capability | Intended experience | Engineering questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversation and voice | Describe a question or task directly | Model quality, voice cost and latency |
| Documents and search | Read files, extract information and retain sources | File limits, retrieval provenance and failures |
| Useful outputs | Lists, drafts, plans and summaries | Quality, editing and export |
| Tasks and memory | Retain approved preferences and task status | Permissions, accuracy, deletion and synchronization |
| Optional identity | Configurable character, voice, name and tone | Asset rights and consistent behavior |

### Example
“Read this event brief, list what I need to prepare this week, draft an announcement, and remind me on Friday to confirm.” The assistant explains its understanding, organizes the material, and produces a checklist and draft. The user confirms saving and reminders. External messages, payments and other consequential actions require confirmation of the specific action and permissions.

### Experience standard
Display an action as completed only after it succeeds. Tool failures should show a reason and next step. A personalized identity can make the product approachable, but cannot replace task quality. Reminders are one capability within the wider everyday task system.


## 04 MVP & delivery scope

Core subscriptions provide useful tasks with explicit cloud allowances and service boundaries.

### Core service
CNY 1/month or 9.90/year includes tasks/lists, personal records, editable plans, permissions, deletion/export and 20 short cloud-text tasks per service month. Broader document, device and automation capabilities expand through validation; native notifications and synchronization require system testing. Personalization purchases do not determine core task quality.

Target service cost per active paid month is CNY 0.10: models 0.06, storage/network 0.02, retries/support 0.02. Twenty short tasks imply a 0.003 model budget each; measure actual context, output and retries. Adjust allowances and routing where usage exceeds cost capacity, with transparent specifications.

Optional packs can provide original identity, themes, tone and licensed voice presets. An illustrative pack costs 19.90, with target fulfillment cost 4. Users do not need a pack to use the assistant. Unlicensed real-person or commercial-character content is excluded.

## 08 Unit economics & viability

### Unit contribution
Each paid service month contributes 1 − 0.10 service − 0.04 processing/tax/refund reserve = CNY 0.86, before one CNY 1 reward and 0.05 growth cost per new payer. Pack contribution is 19.90 − 4 − 19.90 × 4% = 15.104/order.

Five thousand optional packs contribute 75,520, producing 69,048 economic surplus. The 5% attachment rate is an assumption, not observed demand.

At the same cohort timing and costs, about 0.43% pack attachment reaches break-even and 3.74% reaches 50,000 surplus. Without packs, approximately 133,143 new payers reach break-even and 389,185 reach 50,000 surplus. Recalculate when scale changes fixed costs.

Annual unit contribution is 9.90 − 1 reward − 1.20 full-year service − 0.396 reserve − 0.05 growth = 7.254, before allocated fixed costs. This is a full-year illustration, not profit recognized in the quarter of prepayment.

## 10 CNY 1 referrals & the growth model

Continuous referral rewards complement real product value and attributable distribution.

### Eligible referral
Direct link/code attribution → core task → first monthly/annual payment → settlement and refund observation → accrue CNY 1 reward → pay under disclosed terms. Every eligible referral qualifies, without a first-N cap. Self-referrals, duplicate users/purchases, refunded or fraudulent transactions do not count. Personalization purchases are not required for referral eligibility.

### Propagation
K = sharing participation × unique invites × activation × first-payment conversion. 50% × 5 × 50% × 40% = 0.50; 80% × 8 × 50% × 40% = 1.28. Measure real cohorts and generation time, deduplicating overlap with channel acquisition.

### Value and funding
The reward consumes first-payment revenue; renewals and packs contribute later margin. Accrue all eligible referral liabilities. Unpaid rewards and unfulfilled service obligations are not distributable profit. Expansion depends on quality, funding and ability to settle obligations.

## 02 Customers & the problem

Authors, payers and players occupy distinct conversion roles.

| Role | Demand hypothesis | Behavior to validate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Author | Wants personal experience turned into a story with choices | Supplies usable material and confirms characters, outline and facts |

| Player | Enters a friend's story through a link | Starts, reaches an ending and decides whether to create |

### Initial segment

### Behavioral questions

## 03 Product & user journey

Confirm source events and invented branches separately before producing a complete playable story.

Start with adults recording graduation, friendship or career transitions. Anniversary gifts are a testable use case; third-party private information without appropriate consent is not a source. Breakup or traumatic experiences may represent demand, but this trial promises neither therapy nor prediction of another person's reactions.

Text or voice input → extract characters, events and chronology → author confirms fact versus fictional branch → generate and approve outline → generate text and images → check reachable branches and character consistency → review where necessary → private work link → author plays through → author chooses whether to share. Before importing chats, remove names, contact details and irrelevant third-party content. Default to private, without training use.

Example: “Turn my transition from leaving a job to winning my first independent contract into a story with three choices.” Confirm which events happened and which alternatives are imagined. AI-written dialogue must not be presented as a real person's original conversation.
