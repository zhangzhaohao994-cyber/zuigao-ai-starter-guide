# 最高 × Team247 | Economics Prompt V3

Write this prompt before revising the business plans. Preserve the formal paginated website, full English/Chinese editions and English default.

Act as the business analyst, growth planner and financial modeler. Rebuild all three economic models around the updated ambition. Work backward from targets into testable conditions; do not present desired profits as verified forecasts.

1. Personal AI: target at least 100,000 real paying users in 90 days, stretch 200,000. At CNY 1 first payment, revenue is 100,000/200,000. Target a full 50,000 economic budget and 50,000 surplus at the 100,000 stage, including build, labor, models, payment, marketing, rewards and tax/refund reserves. Plan later one-million and ten-million payer stages without invented dates. Define registration, activation, payment and exactly what the entry price buys.
2. Reconcile the referral conflict. Returning CNY 1 on every CNY 1 purchase leaves nothing for other costs. Show that original mechanism's loss as a comparison and propose an explicitly capped first reward experiment. Do not imply the user already agreed to this change: the target requires team acceptance. Do not fill gaps with unsupported subscriptions or sponsorship.
3. Industry agent: offer a higher-value, bounded implementation and monthly service. Show prices, delivery, measurable customer ROI, 90-day revenue recognition and receipt timing. Target six-figure CNY economic surplus; allow conservative losses. The prospect funnel, cycle, reviewer hours and capacity must support the sales plan.
4. Visual novels: combine a larger paid-author base with tiered order value. Define standard/polished specifications, mix, marketing funnel, free sample costs, retries, rights, review and delivery capacity. Target six-figure CNY surplus. Player views are not paying authors.
5. Each proposal needs conservative/target/stretch cases, monthly milestones, full costs, break-even, price/sales/CAC/unit-cost sensitivities, funding and start conditions. Defer unfulfilled revenue; prepayments are not profit. Tax/refund reserves are assumptions, not final net-profit accounting.
6. Label values as targets, budget ceilings, quotation hypotheses or calculated results. Supplier quotations, traffic, conversion, payment, renewal and costs require actual evidence. Macro adoption and competitors do not prove sales.
7. Update linked summary, market, scope, price, cost, acquisition, finance, resources, milestones, metrics and decisions, plus comparison, review responses, downloads and prompt. Earlier low-price/loss models are historical only. Both languages use identical figures and reproducible scripts.
8. Verify all pages, financial identities, language switching, finance interaction, mobile layout and deployment. Do not claim AGI has validated demand or guarantees profitability.
